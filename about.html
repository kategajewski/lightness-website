ABOUT

I'm Kate Gajewski, the heart and hands behind The Lightness of Being, a sacred healing sanctuary nestled in Patchogue, NY. I create a sanctuary where individuals, women in particular, can reconnect with themselves, shed the weight of expectations, and rediscover their most authentic light.

With over eight years of experience as a healer, teacher, and guide, I’ve journeyed through Reiki, sound healing, hypnotherapy, yoga, and more—each modality strengthening my dedication to nurturing holistic transformation. I hold certifications as a Reiki Master in both Usui Ryoho and Holy Fire, alongside mastery in Animal & Pet Reiki, Gong & Sound Resonance work, Clinical Hypnosis and Regression Therapy, as well as a 300-hour Yoga teaching credential. I also serve as an ordained minister, offering deeply meaningful ceremonies such as weddings, Mother Blessingways, and baby blessings.

ROOTS

My path has been enriched by a deep-rooted commitment to community healing, including a decade of service at Pal-O-Mine Equestrian as a healer and facilitator. My background in psychology (BA, University at Albany) and childhood education (MS, Dowling College) infuses my practice with grounded understanding and compassionate insight.

My approach evolves with every shared experience: in 2019, I embraced the transformative power of sound through Gong & Sound Resonance trainings; in 2020, I deepened my embodiment practices with a 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training; and in July 2023, I completed Clinical Hypnotherapy and Past Life Regression certification—guiding clients through inner child healing toward freedom and self-love.
