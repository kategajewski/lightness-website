# Intake Checklist

Gather these before implementation starts.

## Accounts
- Vercel login
- Supabase login
- Stripe login
- Calendly login
- Domain/DNS login
- GoHighLevel login

## Content
- Final page list
- Final offer list
- Final course list
- Final pricing list
- Testimonials
- Contact details
- Legal pages

## Brand Assets
- Logo
- Brand photos
- Preferred colors
- Preferred fonts
- Social links

## Booking + Payments
- Final Calendly URLs
- Stripe product structure
- Recurring vs one-time pricing decisions

## Access Rules
- Which purchases unlock which courses
- Whether subscriptions unlock all content or selected content
- Whether users create accounts before or after checkout
