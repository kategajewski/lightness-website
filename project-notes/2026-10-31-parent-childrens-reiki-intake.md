# Parent intake for Gentle Energy Support

Requested October31,2026: separate intake for a parent or legal guardian to complete for a child receiving Gentle Energy Support (kids Reiki). Based on the existing Reiki intake and private intake infrastructure.

Client path /client-intake/childrens-reiki; API /api/client-intake/childrens-reiki. The parent form supports in-person and virtual sessions via a required format choice. The user was asked the preferred scope; after no reply during independent implementation, both-format coverage was stated in commentary as the working assumption. No booking/reminder automation was changed.

Fields: parent/legal guardian full name, relationship and phone; optional parent email required only for optional parent event updates; child name and age in whole years0–17 (no child email, date of birth or address); intentions, optional comfort/communication needs, previous Reiki and referral. Separate parent authority confirmation, format-specific arrangements, consent and typed parent/guardian signature/date. In-person parents choose hands-off support or light touch subject to the child's comfort. Virtual mode removes and canonicalizes the touch answer. Changing format resets the arrangements confirmation.

Consent respects the child's ability to decline touch, take a break or stop. It retains the existing Reiki medical-care boundary without promising an outcome. No new attendance policy for in-person parents was introduced. Privacy notice reflects the existing private inbox with a required-by-law disclosure boundary. This does not verify guardianship or make legal enforceability claims.

Children's encrypted records use formType=childrens-reiki and snapshot their own consent, guardian authority and selected arrangements. Both earlier signed formats retain their original shape, fingerprints and retries. Shared inbox highlights the child with parent/guardian underneath; private detail uses child-specific labels and guardian signature.
